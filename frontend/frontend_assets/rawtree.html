What we learned about RawTree

[types]

Cast Dynamic columns

Keys of joins, argMax and some aggregates need explicit casts: product_id::String, ts::DateTime64(3).

[connections]

Few at a time

Beyond ~5 parallel connections new ones are refused, so the builds use 2 workers and the service only two connections.

[size]

Split big results

Results are capped at 100 MiB: large aggregates are fetched in hash parts and concatenated.

[nulls]

Empty is not null

In the history clicked is null, in our feedback it is "": we normalise with ifNull(clicked::String, '').
