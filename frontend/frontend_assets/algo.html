How pandabuy chooses ten products

For every request: pre-select up to 300 of the ~5,000 products, score each one, answer the 10 highest in order.

a(user, product) = P(click | examined) · P(purchase | click)  = P(purchase | examined)
One request, end to end~1 ms of compute · answered in ~0.4 s
What we read vs. what we learndata → state → model
Data: RawTree tables State: counters computed from the data Model: weights learned from clicks and purchases
